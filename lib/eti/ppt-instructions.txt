EigenWork: AI candidate evaluation for startups. ETI = EigenTrust Index—evidence-based, risk-aware technical score. Intake: resume + optional GitHub; <2 YOE = junior (code + resume blocks), else senior (resume-only: ops maturity, ownership depth, plausibility). Score 0–85 normalized to 0–100; YOE from resume dates. Overlord: realism check for early talent (YOE≤3), flags inflation/contradictions/ATS slop, can cap at 40 or 46. Matching: job vs candidates by role one_liner (AI: MATCH/ADJACENT/NO); score≥50 + work auth; top 10. Philosophy: risk management over optimistic scoring.
